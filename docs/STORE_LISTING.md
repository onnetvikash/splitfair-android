# Store listing text

**App name:** SplitFair - Bill Splitter

**Short description (80 chars max):**
Split any bill with tip fairly. Free, offline, no ads.

**Full description:**
SplitFair makes splitting the bill at a restaurant, cafe or trip dinner quick and fair.

- Enter the bill and choose a tip from 0 to 30%
- Set how many people are splitting
- Shares are rounded up so the group never comes up short
- Optionally round each share to a whole number for easy cash payments
- Share the result to any chat app in one tap
- Light and dark theme

No sign-up. No ads. No internet or permissions needed. Your data never leaves your phone.

**Category:** Tools (or Finance)
**Content rating:** Everyone
**Privacy policy:** SplitFair collects, stores and shares no user data.
